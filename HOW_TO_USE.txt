HOW TO USE: temporal-pause-breath-architecture
====================================

PURPOSE
  Model the time domain, pause histograms (ms), pause-to-speech ratio,
  inhalation acoustics, intra-phrase velocity/acceleration, and nPVI rhythm metrics.

CAPABILITIES
  - Quantify pause duration histograms (micropause, syntactic, rhetorical ms)
  - Compute total pause-to-speech ratio (%) and turn-latching constants
  - Measure inhalation acoustics: duration (ms), relative SPL (dB), and spectral envelope
  - Calculate normalized Pairwise Variability Index (nPVI) for stress- vs syllable-timing
  - Model intra-phrase velocity curves and phrase-final syllable lengthening (1.2-1.8x)
  - Detail acoustic formant targets and pitch stability of filled pauses ([ʌːm] vs [eː])
  - Output machine-readable temporal_architecture.json

USE CASES
  - "Eliminate the robotic, evenly spaced cadence from this synthetic voice"
  - You want to know exactly how long this speaker pauses for dramatic effect
  - You want to inject audible, natural inhalation sounds before major declarations
  - You need to calculate whether a speaker is stress-timed or syllable-timed

IDEAS
  - Use pause histograms to populate SSML `<break time="...ms"/>` tags
  - Pair with non-speech-vocalization-library for breathing and pre-speech gestures
  - Check nPVI score in clone-verification-match-score

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /temporal-breath-profile Queens-male-orator

  temporal architecture of this speaker

  pause and breath profile for Steve Jobs

  speech timing analysis of this audio clip. Give me pause histogram and nPVI.

  How does this speaker accelerate within a phrase, and where do they inhale?

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Speaking rate (WPM and articulation SPS)
  2. Pause distribution histogram and pause-to-speech ratio
  3. Inhalation duration, intensity, and spectral center
  4. Machine-readable temporal_architecture.json block
